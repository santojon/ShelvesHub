Snapshots are recorded by scripts/ci/report.mjs into site/reports/<scope>/<timestamp>.json and aggregated into site/reports/runs.json. The Ops · Quality reports workflow records a ci snapshot on every push to main, a release snapshot on a published release, and a weekly baseline; run pnpm report for a local one. The dashboard charts these over time.
